Filtering Strategy
Exclude non-primary/unmapped reads
Use SAM flags to remove secondary alignments (-F 256) and unmapped reads (-F 4).

Basic MAPQ filtering
-q 1 removes reads with =1 MAPQ (=79% alignment error probability).

Remove alternative/split alignments
Exclude reads with BWA's XA:Z (alternative hits) and SA:Z (split alignments) tags.
